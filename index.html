Help Center

Guidance and answers for the portal sections available to AI Synthesis users.

Home

The number on the Live Threat Alerts card shows the total alerts currently returned by the alert service. The sidebar badge highlights alerts you have not yet opened.
Use the left navigation or the cards on the Home page. On mobile, open the navigation using the menu button in the top-left corner.

Intelligence Searches

Intelligence Searches is the portal entry point for monitored intelligence records, including the dedicated News, X and Reddit feeds configured for the service.
No result can mean no matching content was collected during the selected monitoring window, the source was temporarily unavailable, or filters narrowed the results. Clear filters and check the feed status before reporting a fault.
Use X for public posts and fast-moving discussion. Use Reddit for community threads and longer-form discussion. Each tab is separated so results can be reviewed and evidenced independently.

Threat Alerts

Alerts are displayed with the most recently posted item first. Severity, category, trajectory and status labels provide quick context for prioritisation.
Opening the Threat Alerts tab marks the current alert set as seen in your browser. The Home card still displays the total current alert count.

Threat Monitoring Reports

Open Threat Monitoring Reports, select the required reporting period, then use the Download button on the report card. Newer reports are shown first.
Upload controls are intended for authorised AI Synthesis users. AI Synthesis users can view and download reports made available through the portal.

Contact B4 Secure

Use it for portal access, missing content, report availability, data-display issues, or general service questions. Include the affected tab, approximate time and a clear description.
Provide the page or tab name, browser, time of issue, expected result, actual result and a screenshot where appropriate. Do not include unnecessary sensitive information.
